Hotel Policies

Important information for your stay

Check-In & Check-Out

Check-In Time

Standard check-in time is 2:00 PM. Early check-in is subject to availability and may incur additional charges. We recommend contacting us in advance if you require early check-in.

Check-Out Time

Standard check-out time is 12:00 PM (noon). Late check-out is available upon request and subject to availability. Additional charges may apply for late check-out beyond 2:00 PM.

Identification Requirements

All guests must present valid government-issued photo identification (passport, driver's license, or national ID card) at check-in. For international guests, a valid passport is required.

Important: The credit card used for booking must be presented at check-in along with valid identification. For third-party bookings, please contact us in advance.

Reservation & Payment

Booking Confirmation

Reservations are confirmed upon receipt of a valid booking confirmation number. We recommend keeping your confirmation email for reference during check-in.

Payment Methods

We accept the following payment methods:

  • Major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express)
  • Debit cards
  • Bank transfers (with advance notice)
  • Cash payments (Nigerian Naira)

Deposit & Guarantee

A credit card authorization or cash deposit may be required at check-in to cover incidental charges. This amount will be released upon check-out after settling all charges.

Cancellation Policy

Standard Cancellation

Cancellations must be made at least 48 hours before the scheduled arrival date to receive a full refund. Cancellations made within 48 hours of arrival will incur a charge equivalent to one night's accommodation.

No-Show Policy

Guests who fail to check in without prior cancellation (no-shows) will be charged the full amount of the reservation.

Special Rates & Packages

Non-refundable rates, promotional packages, and group bookings may have different cancellation policies. Please review the specific terms at the time of booking.

Note: Refunds are processed within 7-14 business days to the original payment method.

Guest Conduct

Quiet Hours

To ensure all guests enjoy a peaceful stay, we observe quiet hours from 10:00 PM to 7:00 AM. We kindly ask guests to be considerate of others during these times.

Smoking Policy

All our properties are non-smoking. Smoking is only permitted in designated outdoor areas. A cleaning fee of ₦50,000 will be charged for smoking in guest rooms or non-designated areas.

Visitors

For security reasons, all visitors must register at the front desk. Visitors are allowed in guest rooms between 8:00 AM and 10:00 PM. Overnight guests must be registered and may incur additional charges.

Prohibited Items & Activities

The following are strictly prohibited on hotel premises:

  • Illegal drugs or substances
  • Weapons of any kind
  • Fireworks or explosives
  • Cooking in guest rooms (except in designated kitchenettes)
  • Disruptive or illegal activities

Children & Extra Guests

Children Policy

Children under 12 years stay free when using existing bedding with parents. Additional bedding for children may incur extra charges.

Maximum Occupancy

Each room has a maximum occupancy limit for safety and comfort. Additional guests beyond the standard occupancy will incur extra charges. Please refer to your room type details for specific occupancy limits.

Cribs & Extra Beds

Cribs and extra beds are available upon request and subject to availability. Please request these at the time of booking or at least 24 hours before arrival.

Pets

We currently do not allow pets at our properties, with the exception of certified service animals. Guests traveling with service animals must notify us at the time of booking and provide appropriate documentation.

Damage & Liability

Property Damage

Guests are responsible for any damage to hotel property caused during their stay. Charges for repairs or replacement will be billed to the guest's account.

Lost & Found

The hotel is not responsible for lost or stolen items. We recommend using in-room safes for valuables. Found items are held for 90 days before disposal.

Hotel Liability

While we take every precaution to ensure guest safety, the hotel is not liable for accidents, injuries, or losses unless directly caused by hotel negligence.

Parking

Complimentary free parking is available for all guests. The hotel is not responsible for damage to or theft from vehicles. Parking is subject to availability.

Internet & Technology

Complimentary high-speed WiFi is provided throughout the hotel. The hotel is not responsible for interruptions in service or any consequences arising from internet use.

Food & Beverage

Outside Food & Beverages

Outside food and beverages are generally not permitted in hotel restaurants and public areas. However, guests may keep personal food items in their rooms.

Room Service

24-hour room service is available. A service charge and delivery fee may apply to room service orders.

Housekeeping

Daily housekeeping service is provided. Guests who prefer not to have daily service can request to opt out at the front desk. "Do Not Disturb" signs should be used to indicate when service is not desired.

Safety & Security

Emergency Procedures

Emergency exit maps are located on the back of each guest room door. In case of emergency, follow staff instructions and use designated emergency exits.

Security Measures

For your safety, we have:

  • 24/7 security personnel
  • CCTV surveillance in public areas
  • Electronic key card access
  • In-room safes
  • Fire safety systems

Special Requests

We strive to accommodate special requests such as room location, floor preference, and accessibility needs. While we cannot guarantee all requests, we will do our best to fulfill them based on availability.

Modifications to Policies

The hotel reserves the right to modify these policies at any time. Guests will be notified of any significant changes that may affect their reservation.
